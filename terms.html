These terms explain what happens before, during and after a booking with Bahadur Tours & Travels. Please read the package-specific quotation together with this page.

01

Booking & payment

  • A booking request is not a confirmed reservation. Confirmation is issued in writing after availability is checked and the required deposit or full payment is received.
  • The lead traveller must provide accurate names, ages, contact details and document information for every guest.
  • Quotations are valid only for the period shown and may change when airline, hotel, permit, tax or currency costs change.
  • Payment may be accepted by bank transfer, UPI or another method shown on the official invoice. Never send funds to an unverified personal account.
  • Late or incomplete payment may release reserved services without liability to Bahadur Tours & Travels.
02

Cancellation & refunds

Cancellation requests must be sent from the booking contact by email or WhatsApp and are effective only after acknowledgement.

Before supplier confirmationRefundable less payment gateway or bank charges.
After confirmationSupplier cancellation charges plus Bahadur service charges apply.
No-show or unused serviceNormally non-refundable.
  • Package-specific cancellation terms in the quotation take priority where they are stricter or more detailed.
  • Refunds are returned to the original payment method after suppliers release funds. Processing times depend on banks and providers.
  • Visa rejection, illness, weather disruption and personal emergencies do not automatically create a right to a full refund; travel insurance is strongly recommended.
03

Travel documents & traveller responsibilities

  • Travellers are responsible for passports, visas, permits, identification, health certificates and entry requirements.
  • Names provided for tickets must exactly match official documents. Correction and reissue costs are paid by the traveller.
  • Bahadur Tours & Travels is not responsible for denied boarding or entry caused by missing, expired or incorrect documents.
  • Travellers must follow local laws, customs, hotel rules and the lawful instructions of guides and authorities.
04

Changes, substitutions & force majeure

  • Reasonable itinerary changes may be made for safety, weather, transport disruption, government restrictions or supplier availability.
  • Where a major confirmed service becomes unavailable, we will aim to provide a comparable alternative or explain the available refund or credit.
  • Bahadur is not liable for events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, strikes, epidemics, civil unrest, closures and carrier cancellations.
05

Liability & third-party providers

  • Hotels, airlines, ferries, activity operators and vehicle providers are independent suppliers responsible for their own services.
  • Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded under Indian law.
  • Travellers remain responsible for valuables, medication and personal belongings. Report loss, injury or service issues immediately so assistance can be attempted.
06

Accommodation, transport & special requests

  • Hotel categories and room descriptions follow supplier information. Check-in, meals, bedding and local taxes vary by property.
  • Vehicle type may be replaced with a comparable vehicle when operationally necessary.
  • Accessibility, dietary, adjoining-room and other special requests are not guaranteed unless confirmed in writing.
07

Activities, health & safety

  • Adventure and water activities involve inherent risk. Guests must disclose relevant medical conditions and follow safety briefings.
  • Operators may refuse participation where age, fitness, weather, equipment or conduct creates a safety risk.
  • Guests must not participate while impaired by alcohol or drugs. Appropriate travel and medical insurance is recommended.
08

Privacy & communications

  • We use personal information to quote, book, support and communicate about travel services, issue invoices and meet legal obligations.
  • Necessary data may be shared with travel suppliers, payment providers, WhatsApp, email providers and authorities. We do not sell personal information.
  • Operational records are retained only as reasonably required for bookings, accounting, disputes and law. Access to the operations dashboard must be restricted to authorised staff.
  • Marketing messages include an opt-out. Essential booking and safety messages may still be sent.
09

Reviews, photos & visitor videos

  • Guests keep ownership of submitted reviews and media but grant Bahadur permission to display approved submissions on its website and marketing channels.
  • Do not upload content featuring people who have not agreed to publication, children without guardian permission, copyrighted music or unlawful material.
  • Submissions may be moderated, cropped or removed for safety, privacy, accuracy and presentation. Permission can be withdrawn for future use by contacting us.
10

Intellectual property, complaints & law

  • Bahadur branding, original website content and layouts may not be commercially copied without permission.
  • Report service complaints as soon as possible during the trip and send supporting details within 14 days after travel.
  • These terms are governed by Indian law. Courts with competent jurisdiction in India will hear unresolved disputes.
