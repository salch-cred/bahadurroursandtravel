Sample itinerary

Your sacred journey, day by day

Based on the 12-day Standard package. Custom itineraries are available.

1

Departure from Kerala

Assemble at Kochi or Calicut airport. Flight to Jeddah. Airport welcome and transfer to your Makkah hotel.

2–3

Arrival in Makkah: Tawaf and Sa’i

Check in near Masjid Al-Haram. Perform Umrah rituals: Tawaf, Sa’i between Safa and Marwa, and halq or taqsir.

4–5

Makkah Ziyarat

Guided visit to Jabal Al-Nour, Jabal Thawr, Mina, Muzdalifah and the Arafat plains.

6–7

Travel to Madinah

Comfortable coach or private car journey to Madinah. Check in near Masjid An-Nabawi and visit the Prophet’s Mosque in the evening.

8–9

Madinah Ziyarat

Visit Masjid Quba, Masjid Al-Qiblatain, Uhud Mountain, the dates market and historical sites.

10–11

Free days for ibadah and shopping

Personal time for prayers and for gifts, dates and itr. Guidance is available as needed.

12

Return to Kerala

Transfer to Jeddah or Madinah airport. Fly back to Kochi or Calicut with Zam Zam water and blessings.
