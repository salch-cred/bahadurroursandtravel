Sample itinerary

Your sacred journey, day by day

Based on 12-day Standard package. Custom itineraries available.

1

Departure from Kerala

Assemble at Kochi/Calicut airport. Flight to Jeddah. Airport welcome and transfer to Makkah hotel.

2–3

Arrival in Makkah — Tawaf & Sa'i

Check-in near Masjid Al-Haram. Perform Umrah rituals — Tawaf (circling Kaaba 7 times), Sa'i between Safa and Marwa, halq/taqsir.

4–5

Makkah Ziyarat

Guided visit to Jabal Al-Nour (Cave Hira), Jabal Thawr, Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafat plains — all blessed locations of Islamic history.

6–7

Travel to Madinah

Comfortable coach/private car journey to Madinah. Check-in to hotel near Masjid An-Nabawi. Evening visit to the Prophet's Mosque.

8–9

Madinah Ziyarat

Visit Masjid Quba (first mosque in Islam), Masjid Al-Qiblatain, Uhud Mountain, dates market and historical sites.

10–11

Free days for ibadah & shopping

Personal time for prayers, shopping for gifts, dates, itr (perfume) and mementos. Guidance available as needed.

12

Return to Kerala

Transfer to Jeddah/Madinah airport. Flight back to Kochi/Calicut. Arrive with Zam Zam water and blessings.
